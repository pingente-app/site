Frequently asked questions

The screen flashed. Is that normal?

Yes. E-ink flashes black and white on every face change — it is part of the technology. After that, the image stays fixed without using the battery.

Does it work without internet?

The Pingente does: the image is stored in it. The phone needs internet only for faces that fetch data, such as weather and events.
