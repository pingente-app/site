Pingente in one minute

Screen recording of the app on a Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra with a real e-ink tag nearby (no login, no account):

  1. Home: known tags, battery level and the last face sent.
  2. Tag detail: current face and the grid of faces.
  3. Note face: text + icon, saved.
  4. "Send to keychain": the app connects over Bluetooth, uploads the image and the tag refreshes (10–20 s).
  5. Notification shade: the persistent notification of the automatic proximity sync (foreground service, type connectedDevice).
